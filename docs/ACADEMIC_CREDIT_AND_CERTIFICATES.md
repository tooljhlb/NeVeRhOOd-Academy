# Academic credits, certificates and credentials

NeVeRhOOd Academy is an independent, **non-accredited** learning-resource directory and curriculum project.

## What the Academy can offer

- Original courses, lessons, software projects, quizzes, and self-assessments.
- Independent completion records and downloadable **NeVeRhOOd Academy** certificates for courses the Academy actually offers and evaluates.
- Curated pathways to official providers' coursework and assessments, with direct source links.
- Advanced study and research-oriented tracks inspired by university-level topics.

## What the Academy does not currently offer

- Accredited college/university credits, transcripts, ECTS units, CEUs, or institutional qualifications.
- A Bachelor's, Master's, PhD, or doctoral degree, or any guaranteed equivalence to one.
- Certificates on behalf of Harvard, MIT, Google, Microsoft, or any other external provider.
- Automated completion of third-party exams or academic assessments in violation of those providers' policies.

## Catalog verification fields

Each proposed resource must have provider, title, original official URL, subject, level, verified link status, registration/price, assessment type, certificate award availability, and explicit credit type/quantity (only with an official citation). If the official provider does not confirm an attribute, it remains **UNKNOWN**.

Repeated links and generic university department homepages must not be represented as distinct direct tests.

## Educational credentials

A completion badge from NeVeRhOOd Academy proves only the stated Academy-defined requirements were met under its documented process. It does not imply academic accreditation or endorsement.
