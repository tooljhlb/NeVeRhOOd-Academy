How AI usage and billing would work

The Academy membership is separate from model inference. Learners may eventually connect their own supported AI provider account, where the provider bills that learner. NeVeRhOOd will not promise unlimited model tokens within a $10 membership. Captain AEGIS is a separate original research checkpoint with verified GPU experiments; a continuously available hosted Captain service is not yet offered and would require separate compute pricing.

Independent learning
